# Review

- [Architecture-practice reviews user guide](architecture-practice-reviews-user-guide.md) - Every command, key, table, and refusal the guide names exists and behaves as written, but the guide presents a resolution path with no production ingress and a profile-provisioning step with no procedure as if an operator could perform them today.
- [Improvement-requests OKF corpus before the reviews bundle joins it](improvement-requests-okf-corpus.md) - The nine requests validate strictly and their log and index are current, but the bundle pins a profile three catalog releases behind the one the new reviews bundle pins, the request the landed review flow fulfils is still proposed, and the Mori registry entry that should resolve their URIs is stale.
- [OpenTelemetry practice-review specialist Flow](opentelemetry-practice-review-flow.md) - The Flow dispatches strictly, replaces every trusted field before validation, and orders memory before history before attention as designed; its failure path is crash-shaped rather than terminal, its problem claims are rendered with derived Show, and a producer-named prior review is retired without a relationship check.
- [ExecPlan 46 closeout against the landed tree](plan-46-review-orchestration-closeout.md) - The plan's reproducible gates hold at the reviewed commit, but its closeout claims end-to-end coverage the conformance suite does not contain, and three documents it owns were left stale.
- [Practice-review attention migration](practice-review-attention-migration.md) - The additive migration creates exactly the two logical tables ADR 0035 specifies, carries no delivery state, and applies twice cleanly; it lacks only an index for the audit query that walks from a review id to its reservation.
- [Practice-review attention reservation](practice-review-attention-reservation.md) - The atomic reservation is correct under the concurrent race it was built for, but a replay after a journaled skip can commit a reservation whose outbox message never existed, and the semantic key includes a model-chosen claim id.
- [Practice-review capability ports and runtime adapters](practice-review-capability-ports.md) - The five narrow effects keep IO in interpreters and behind fixed journal steps, the recipient map is canonicalized and boot-validated, and the model evaluator reuses the profile-constrained environment; the leftover non-atomic attention interpreter and an unjournaled evaluator failure are the only rough edges.
- [Practice-review resolution authorization boundary](practice-review-resolution-authorization.md) - The envelope binds a verified subject to a resolution and survives replay without a second authorizer call, but nothing in the runtime asks whether that subject is entitled to resolve this review, and the opaque credential type derives Show.
