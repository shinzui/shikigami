Usage: shikigami proposals COMMAND

  Read proposals, candidates and admissions, and install or revoke standing
  policies

Available options:
  -h,--help                Show this help text

Available commands:
  policy                   Install, revoke and list standing policies as the
                           configured operator
  list                     Page through proposals
  inspect                  Show observer and policy usage with exhaustion
                           explicit
  candidates               Page through an observer's recorded candidates
  admissions               Page through admissions pinned to their policy
                           digests
  prune                    Delete terminal skips older than an instant, a batch
                           at a time
